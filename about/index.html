A NOTE FROM CLARA

Welcome. I'm glad
you found your way here.

Clara Stone writes clean, atmospheric cozy mysteries filled with seaside charm, inviting cafés, close-knit communities, clever clues and secrets waiting just beneath the surface.

She is the author of the Shoreline Inn & Café Mysteries, set in the coastal town of Seastar Bay, where warm hospitality, fresh pastries and beautiful ocean views are never far from an unexpected mystery.

Her second series, A Bluebird Bakery Mystery, welcomes readers to Silverlake, Vermont, and the comforts of a friendly bakery where everyday life always has room for a little intrigue.

✦

Why the pen name?

Clara Stone is a pen name. It offers the freedom to share stories and ideas with readers while keeping the author's private life private. The name is a home for the books; the stories are the part she hopes you'll carry with you.

Whether you're stopping in for your next mystery or just a quiet moment among the books, you're welcome here.

Clara Stone — with warm wishes
