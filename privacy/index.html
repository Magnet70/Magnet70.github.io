Who operates this website

This website represents Clara Stone, a pen name used for cozy mystery writing. For correspondence, email clara@clarastonebooks.com.

Newsletter and Reader Club

The Clara Stone Reader Club is operated through MailerLite. When you subscribe through the live signup form, MailerLite processes the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, to deliver welcome messages, reader gifts and future email updates. You can unsubscribe from those messages using the link provided in the emails. The website's current on-site email field is only a disabled placeholder and collects no data.

External services

This website is designed to be hosted with GitHub Pages. Its pages may request external font files from Google Fonts, and its outbound links lead to services such as Amazon, YouTube, Facebook and Goodreads. Those services have their own privacy practices. When the MailerLite signup embed is added, it may load additional MailerLite resources.

Amazon affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some book links on this website are affiliate links (paid links). Clara Stone may earn a small commission if you make a qualifying purchase after using one of those links, without changing your purchase price.

No payment processing

This website does not accept payments or collect payment card details. Purchases, if you choose to make one, are completed on third-party retailers' websites.

Contact and updates

For questions about this notice or author correspondence, write to clara@clarastonebooks.com. This notice may be updated as the site's services change.
